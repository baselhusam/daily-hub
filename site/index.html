DailyHub developer preview

The day, in one place.

DailyHub is a personal command center for people who juggle more than one project at a time — founders, consultants, indie builders, anyone with client work, side projects, and a few non-negotiable daily habits.

It is not a replacement for Linear, Jira, or Notion. It is the morning surface: what is open, what belongs where, what must happen today, and what you already finished.
