Rules
the short version
ALWAYS
Accent means live: today, streak, link, focus.
Green means completed. Red means late.
Separate with a 1px rule before reaching for a shadow.
Numbers are tabular and right-aligned in lists.
NEVER
No new colors, no gradients, no nested cards.
No accent fills larger than a button.
No modal for anything that can be an inline row.
No spinner where a skeleton row will do.
WHEN IN DOUBT
Remove it and see if today still reads at a glance.
The product's job is to make one day legible. Anything that competes with that is decoration.
